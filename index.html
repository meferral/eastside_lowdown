About the Band

East Nashville’s own gypsy jazz quartet

Eastside Lowdown plays swing era tunes, specializing in the music of Django Reinhardt. We also bring a repertoire of contemporary music played in the swing idiom, as well as original tunes. We’re a quartet available for private events and large public events alike.

Eastside Lowdown was born from the Nashville Django Jam and calls East Nashville home.
